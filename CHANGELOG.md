# Changelog

All notable changes to xNBTD are documented in this file.

## [2.1.0] - 2026-07-02

### Changed
- Added GLS collection points tracking (`Points ramasses`) and hid deprecated `AVP Relais` and `SHD` columns (`99359f0`).
- Updated operational planning views and dependencies (`2025-04-13` to `2025-04-25`).

## [2.0.0] - 2025-01-04

### Changed
- Upgraded Django models, admin interfaces, and Poetry dependencies (`925863f`, `12fd9f4`).
- Added route management templates and shift planning enhancements.

## [1.0.9] - 2023-10-26

### Fixed
- Consolidated v1.0.x stability fixes across tour schedules and driver assignments (`d04d2e1`).
